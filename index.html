01 — The brief

A new mark
for GENZA

GENZA is a Tunisian streetwear label built by two — a small drop-driven brand with a bold voice: Dream. Hustle. Repeat. The work so far lives on Instagram, on tees, flags and pop-up spaces.

The ask: a new logo and a cleaner identity system — one mark that works small on a label, big on a flag, and everywhere in between.

This presentation gathers the full picture: where the brand started, the new logo system, and how it lives in the wild.
